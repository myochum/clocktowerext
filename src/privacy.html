Clocktower Twitch Extension – Privacy Notice

Effective date: 2026-01-20

Summary

This Twitch Extension does not collect, store, or transmit personal data from viewers.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect:

  • Twitch usernames, user IDs, email addresses, or messages
  • OAuth tokens
  • Device identifiers
  • IP addresses or precise location

Analytics & tracking

We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs.

Identity Linking (ID Share)

We do not use Twitch Identity Linking / ID Share.

Sharing

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any.
